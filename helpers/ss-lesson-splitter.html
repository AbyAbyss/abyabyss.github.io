Automatic converts legacy Malayalam fonts directly, and switches to OCR when the PDF's own Malayalam text is damaged or missing.

PDF support needs an internet connection: it loads PDF.js and, for OCR, a reading model of a few MB the first time. Bold, sizes and layout are approximated, so proofread the result before printing.
