              How it works

              A bigger drive doesn't mean a bigger app.

              The scan tree lives in a SQLite file on disk, never in memory. Every screen asks the database a small question and lets go of the answer. Quit and relaunch, and you land back on your last scan instantly.

              The small print

              Everything else, briefly.

              The parts that don't fit in a window demo.

              Private by design

              Runs entirely on your Mac. No telemetry, no sync, no account. Scan data lives under ~/Library/Application Support/wimys_crumb/.

              Full Disk Access

              Grant it to see everything. Without it the app still works, skips the locked-down areas, and shows a banner saying how many folders it couldn't read.

              Any folder, not just drives

              Start lists mounted volumes and recent scans. Prefer a single folder? Use "Pick a specific folder".

              Scheduled scans

              Pick how often and optionally scan only on power. They run when the app is opened, not in the background.

              Snapshots

              Past scan databases can be browsed and deleted from Settings, or cleared all at once.

              JSON export

              Settings can export your scan as JSON, if you want the numbers somewhere else.

              Install

              Free to a good Mac.

              Grab the DMG from the Releases page, drag it to Applications, approve it once. Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. License: to be decided; the source is public on GitHub.

              1. Download and dragGet wimys_crumb-<version>.dmg, open it and drag wimys_crumb into Applications.
              2. "macOS can't verify it"It's a free side project and isn't notarized. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, or run the command on the right.
              3. Let it see everythingGrant Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security. It works without, and tells you how many folders it skipped.
