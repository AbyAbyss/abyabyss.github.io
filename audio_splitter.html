How it works, and what to expect

This is classic signal processing, not an AI model, so it runs offline with no model download. Vocals are found by comparing the left and right channels: anything panned dead center in the vocal frequency range is treated as voice. Drums and melodic parts are separated by their shape on the spectrogram (sustained tones versus short hits). Bass is the sustained low end. The vocals and the music always add back up to the original.

It works best on stereo songs where the singer is centered, which is most pop and film music. Expect some leftover bleed: a centered snare or lead guitar can end up in the vocals, and reverb tails on the voice stay in the music. Mono files cannot be split into vocals this way. AI separators such as Demucs give cleaner results, but need a model of a few hundred MB.
