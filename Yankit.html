Capabilities

Everything you copy.
Nothing you'll lose.

Yankit quietly watches your clipboard and keeps a searchable history of every text snippet, image, and file you copy.

Captures everything

Plain text, rich text, images, and files — all recorded automatically the moment you copy them.

Instant search

Press ⌘⇧V, start typing. The history filters live as you type — find anything in milliseconds.

Paste straight back

Select any item and Yankit pastes it directly into the app you were working in. One keypress.

Pin what matters

Pinned items are exempt from the rolling limit and never auto-deleted. Keep your essentials forever.

Light on memory

History lives in SQLite with image blobs on disk, not in RAM. Idles at ~40 MB — barely a whisper.

Privacy-first

Skips password-manager fields, dims while paused, and keeps data out of Spotlight and Time Machine.

Pause anytime

One click silences capture for an hour, until tomorrow, or until you resume. Full control.

Per-app exclusions

Tell Yankit to ignore copies made in specific apps. Granular privacy where you need it.

Architecture

Two paths.
One shared clipboard.

Yankit lives in the menu bar — no Dock icon, no window in your way. Lightweight, honest, fast.

A lightweight monitor watches the system pasteboard and records each new copy into a local SQLite database, with image and file payloads stored as files on disk so the database stays small and fast.

The capture path records what you copy. The recall path puts it back. When you pick an item, it goes back on the pasteboard and Yankit pastes it into wherever you were.

Nothing leaves your Mac — no account, no sync, no telemetry. Your clipboard history is yours alone.

Installation

Build and run
in two commands.

The Xcode project is generated from project.yml via XcodeGen, so it's not committed to the repo.

Terminal
# Prerequisites
brew install xcodegen
 
# Generate and open the project
xcodegen generate
open Yankit.xcodeproj
 
# Press ⌘R to run, ⌘U to test

Accessibility Permission Required

Grant Yankit Accessibility access when prompted (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility) for one-touch paste. Without it, Yankit still places your selection on the clipboard for a manual ⌘V.
