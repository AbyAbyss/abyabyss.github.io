What it does

Shows, draws, gets it done

Most AI help lives in a chat window you have to switch to. Helpy sits by your cursor instead. Ask it something about the app in front of you and it answers on that app, with marks you can follow. Ask it to do something and it plans the work, asks before anything risky, and keeps going while you carry on.

Walkthroughs

It shows you where to click

Ask "how do I turn on dark mode?" and Helpy highlights the real control, one step at a time. Click it, or press Next, and Helpy takes a fresh screenshot to check before it shows the next step.

Highlights snap onto the actual button through your computer's accessibility layer, so a slightly-off guess still lands.

"Do it" clicks it for you, after you say OK

It draws to explain

Stuck on a diagram, a chart or a lecture video? Helpy traces over it: lines, shapes and curves with short labels, drawn one after another while the buddy moves the pen.

Background agents

It does the task while you keep working

Say "clean up my desktop" or "research standing desks under £400". Helpy plans one to five agents and shows you a plan card first: each agent's goal, its tools, and what it will ask before acting.

Every agent gets a glowing chip at the edge of your screen. Hover it to see what it's doing, answer its questions, or approve its next step. Finished work comes with suggested next steps and a follow-up bar.

Every file change is backed up, and a whole run can be undone in one click.

Deletes, sends and commands wait for your OK.

From one sentence

How a request becomes an agent

You can start agents by voice, by typing, from a circled part of the screen, or from a template. Whichever way you start, it goes like this.

  1. 1

    Say it

    Hold the hotkey and ask in your own words. No commands to learn.

    "sort my downloads"
  2. 2

    Check the plan

    The plan card lists each agent, its tools and what it will ask first. Press Enter, click Start, or say "yes, go".

  3. 3

    Watch the dock

    A copy of the buddy flies to the screen edge and becomes the agent's chip.

  4. 4

    Keep it or undo it

    Read the result on its card, ask a follow-up, or roll back every file change at once.
