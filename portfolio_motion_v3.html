About

My whole career, 8 years across four startups, has been owning products end to end: a Zero Trust security product built from nothing to 10+ enterprise customers, a company-wide reporting platform, and production LLM and MCP infrastructure.

I'm the person people bring "could we actually do this?" to, before anyone spends money finding out. At every company I've worked at, product managers, architects and sales brought me ideas to assess, and my proofs of concept repeatedly became the base implementation months later.

I work mostly in TypeScript and Node.js, with Go and Python where they fit, across NestJS services, event-driven systems on AWS, and MCP and LLM platforms. I like pairing leadership with hands-on work: mentoring a team, setting the review standards, and building things that scale and keep working.
